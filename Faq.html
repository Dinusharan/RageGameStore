Frequently Asked Questions

How can I create an account on your website?

You can create an account by clicking on the 'Sign Up' button and filling in your details. A confirmation email will be sent for verification.

How do I check the status of my order?

Once your order is placed, you can check its status under the 'My Orders' section of your account.

Can I cancel or modify my order after placing it?

Orders can be canceled or modified within 24 hours of placement. Contact our support team for assistance.

Do you offer refunds or exchanges on games?

Yes, we offer refunds and exchanges under specific conditions. Please check our refund policy for more details.

My game isn't working. What should I do?

First, check if your system meets the game's requirements. If the issue persists, visit our support page or contact customer service.

How can I contact customer support for assistance?

You can reach us through our 'Contact Us' page or email us at support@ragegamestore.com.
